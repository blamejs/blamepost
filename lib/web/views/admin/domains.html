Adding a domain does not open the MX listener for it. Publish MX, SPF, DMARC, the selector records, MTA-STS and TLS-RPT, then confirm the posture above.

Confirming the posture is your statement that the records exist. It is not a check of them. Run blamepost doctor to resolve each one and see what is actually published.
